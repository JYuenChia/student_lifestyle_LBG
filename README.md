#  Mental Health Support for Students (Mobile Application)

## 📌 Track & Problem Statement
**Track:** Track 1 – Student Lifestyle  

**Problem Statement:**  
**Mental Health Support for Students (Mobile Application)**  

**Background:**  
University students today face growing mental health challenges such as anxiety and depression, often brought on by financial stress, academic pressure, and the demands of campus life.  

**The Problem:**  
Many students don’t seek help because counseling resources are limited, or they fear stigma. This challenge will explore creating tools for self-care, mental health support, and helping students cope with the demands of university life. 

**The Solution:**  
We propose a **mobile-first application tailored for university students** that directly addresses academic stress, emotional struggles, and the need for peer support.  

Instead of providing only counselling referrals, our app empowers students to:  
- **Reflect on their emotions** through guided mood journaling.  
- **Build support networks** via anonymous sharing, group chats, and goal-based collaboration (community).  
- **Stay organized and resilient** with to-do lists, reminders, and progress tracking.  

By combining **self-care, peer connection, and academic management tools**, the solution complements existing university counselling services while fostering a safe, stigma-free environment where students can thrive.

## 📱 App Structure & Features

### 1. Home / Dashboard
- Quick access to main tools (**Calendar, Mood Analysis, Period Tracker, My Journal, Settings**)  
- Daily **motivational / encouragement message**  

---

### 2. Mood Diary
- Choose preset emoji OR draw mood manually  
- can write journal
- a calendar that provides visual mood tracking and journaling per day.

---

### 3. Discussion
**a. Seek Help / Give Support**  
- Discussion area for students to share diaries  
- Ask questions about stress & receive supportive comments  
- Can request follow and chat with other users
- Can check favourites and posts in the personal profile page 

**b. Community Goals (Study Buddy)**  
- Students create personal goals (e.g., *“Finish Lab Report”*)  
- Option: *Allow others to join* (public by default, can be set private)  
- Public community will be shown in the community page
- Other users can choose to join their interested community 
- After joining, a **group chat is auto-created** in the Messages section  

**c. Profile Page**
- Display profile photo, username, and personal status/bio  
- Manage mood (dropdown for daily mood selection)  
- View communities joined  
- Track statistics: posts, likes, followers, following  
- Tabs for Posts and Favourites  
- Edit username and bio  


---

### 4. Mood Analysis
- Mood chart (**daily / weekly / monthly**)  
- Longest positive streak (e.g., *“7 days happy chain”*)  
- Insights on mood trends  
- Daily/Weekly/Monthly mood summary + streak highlights  
- **Period Tracker (for female users only)**  
  - Tracking period of different Separate menstrual health section  
  - A calendar for visual mood, symptoms, and notes tracking per day..
  - Insights on mood trends for each menstrual health section  

---

### 5. Messages
- Same Goal Group Chat from community→ discussion space for students in shared goals  
- Private Messaging → 1-to-1 conversations with peers/friends  
- Request to chat → user can choose to accept or reject (if they accept, the new chat will be added to the chat list, else the request will be deleted)
- Features →  red dot on profile picture for unseen chat, read receipt (single tick represents unseen, double ticks represent seen), recommended positive message

---

### 6. To-Do List
- Create task cards which are well separated based on categories (**Academic, Personal, Wellness**)  
- Add deadlines and repetition (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly) with reminders  
- Passed deadline tasks will be shown in red for better visibility
- **Focus Mode**, with a timer that has been pre-set to 25 minutes following the Pomodoro method (but the timer can be adjusted), for better focus with motivational quotes

---

### 7. Settings
- Edit profile picture, username & choose their university 
- Direct access to **Counselling Hotline**  of their university
- Accessibility features (font size, colorblind mode, dark mode, reminders)  
- Privacy settings  
- Log out
